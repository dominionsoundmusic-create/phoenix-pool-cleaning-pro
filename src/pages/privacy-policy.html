Who this policy covers

Phoenix Pool Cleaning Pro is a d/b/a of Dominion Digital Group. It runs the website phoenixpoolcleaningpro.com and the phone line published on it. It is a free referral line, not a pool company: it answers calls and transfers callers to independent, locally owned pool service companies. In this policy, "the line" means Phoenix Pool Cleaning Pro, and "pool company" means an independent business you are connected with.

What the line collects when you call

An automated assistant answers each call. To connect you, it collects what you choose to say, which usually includes:

  • your name and the phone number you are calling from or would like a call back on;
  • the town, neighborhood or general area of the pool;
  • a short description of the pool problem or the service you want.

The phone system also creates a record of the call, such as the time, length and calling number. The assistant turns what you say into the short summary the pool company hears before it picks up.

What the website collects

The website has no forms and does not ask you for personal information. Like most websites, the hosting service may log basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time. If you use the EN/ES language toggle, the page loads Google's translation tool and stores a small cookie that remembers your language choice; Google's own privacy terms apply to that tool.

How the information is used

  • To transfer your call to an independent pool company that takes calls for your area, and to give that company a short summary before it answers.
  • To pass your name, callback number, area and pool problem to that company if it cannot answer, so it can call you back.
  • To run, secure and improve the phone line and the website, and to keep records the business needs.
  • To meet legal obligations or respond to lawful requests.

Who receives it

The details from your call go to the pool company you are connected with. Once it has them, that company handles them under its own policies, because it is a separate business. The line also uses service providers, such as phone, call handling and website hosting providers, that process information only to provide those services. The line does not sell your personal information.

Text messages

The line does not send marketing text messages. A pool company you are connected with may contact you by phone or text about your request; ask that company about its own messaging and opt-out practices.

How long it is kept and how it is protected

Call records are kept only as long as reasonably needed to operate the line, handle follow-up questions and meet legal or accounting needs. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no phone or internet system is completely secure.

Your choices

You can decide what to say on a call, and you can hang up at any time. To ask about the information the line holds about a call you made, or to ask for it to be corrected or deleted where the law allows, call the line at {{ site.business.phone_display }} and leave your request. To ask a pool company about information it holds, contact that company directly.

Children

The line and website are meant for adults arranging pool service for a property. They are not directed to children under 13, and the line does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

This policy may change as the line changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. This page describes the line's practices; it is not legal advice.
