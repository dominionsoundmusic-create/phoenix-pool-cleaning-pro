Pool service and repair in Phoenix covers two kinds of work: keeping the water clean and balanced every week, and fixing the equipment and surfaces that heat, sun, hard water and dust wear out. Both are handled by the independent pool company you reach through the line, never by the line itself.

Below, every service has its own page explaining what the job involves, what a careful pool technician checks and what to ask before you approve the work. If you are not sure where to start, the table matches common problems to the right page.

Cleaning and water care

Routine work that keeps the water clear and safe through Phoenix heat, sun and dust. In Arizona this is generally not treated as contracting work, so it usually does not call for an ROC license.
