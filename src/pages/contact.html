Why phone only?

A form puts you in a queue. A call puts you on the line with a pool company, usually in a minute or two, and if it cannot pick up, it gets your details for a call back. That is the whole idea of the line.

Not sure what to say? Start with what you see

You do not need pool vocabulary. "The water turned green after the storm," "the pump hums but nothing moves," or "the heater clicks and shuts off" is plenty. If the pump or heater is making a burning smell or tripping a breaker, turn it off at the controller or breaker and leave it off until a technician looks. If you smell gas near the heater, get away from it first and call 911 and Southwest Gas from a safe place.
