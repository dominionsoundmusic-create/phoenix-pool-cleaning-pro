Check the license first. Arizona's Registrar of Contractors (ROC) licenses contractors, and its residential classifications include R-6, Swimming Pool Service and Repair. Repairs, equipment replacement and resurfacing generally call for a licensed contractor; whether a cleaning-only service needs a license is not spelled out, so ask the ROC if it matters to you. Ask the company for its ROC license number and look it up on the ROC contractor search (opens in a new tab) before work begins. The line does not check this for you.
