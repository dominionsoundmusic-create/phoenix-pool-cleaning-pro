About Phoenix Pool Cleaning Pro

Local Technicians, the Hardest Water in Arizona

Phoenix Pool Cleaning Pro is a residential pool service company covering the entire Phoenix metro — central Phoenix, North Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Glendale, Peoria, and Surprise. We handle weekly cleaning, water chemistry, equipment repair, and tile restoration for pools running in 300 to 900 ppm calcium water — the hardest municipal supply in the state.

We are not a franchise and we are not a scheduling app that sends a different person every week. The same technician services your pool every visit, knows your equipment, knows your water, and notices when something changes before it becomes a repair bill.

What We Do

Weekly pool cleaning with chemicals included — skimming, brushing, vacuuming, chemistry testing and balancing, filter and equipment checks. One flat monthly price, no per-visit charges.

Equipment repair — pumps, motors, filters, heaters, salt cells, lights, and plumbing leaks. Diagnosed on site, priced before work starts.

Tile cleaning — calcium scale removal by media blasting. No drain, no glaze damage, swimmable the same day.

One-time deep cleans — for pools that have been sitting, just purchased, or reopening after the snowbird season.

Why Phoenix Is Different

Phoenix tap water runs 300 to 900 parts per million calcium hardness — two to four times what equipment manufacturers recommend. That means every pool in the Valley is scaling its heaters, salt cells, and tile from the day it fills. Add year-round pool season, summer highs above 115 degrees that double chlorine demand, and monsoon dust storms that load filters in a single pass, and you have a climate that punishes pool equipment harder than almost any other metro in the country.

Our chemistry approach, our equipment inspection checklist, and our maintenance intervals are all built for this reality — not for national averages that franchise operations import from milder markets.
