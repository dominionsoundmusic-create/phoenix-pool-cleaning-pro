Surprise's Water

Surprise sits on the far west side of the Valley and has grown rapidly over the past two decades, which means its water infrastructure serves a mix of brand-new master-planned communities and established neighborhoods. The city draws from Central Arizona Project canal water, Salt River Project deliveries, and local groundwater wells. Calcium hardness typically runs 350 to 600 ppm — the northern developments near Sun City Grand and Waddell tend to run at the high end because of heavier groundwater reliance.

At these levels, calcium deposits on everything the water touches. Tile scales in 12 to 18 months. Heater heat exchangers crust and lose efficiency. Salt cell plates coat and reduce chlorine output. Weekly chemistry monitoring is the only way to track it and adjust before equipment damage begins.

Surprise Neighborhoods We Serve

Marley Park

One of the most recognized master-planned communities in the West Valley — a New Urbanist design with porches, parks, and a neighborhood feel that draws families. Homes built from the mid-2000s onward with pools now in the 10-to-20-year age range where original equipment starts needing attention. Marley Park pools are typically mid-size with standard chlorine or salt systems.

Sun City Grand

The Del Webb 55-plus community in northwest Surprise. We service private residential pools here — not the HOA community pools. Many Sun City Grand homes have smaller pools or spas that are used year round, and the owners are often snowbirds who leave the pool unattended for part of the year. We offer year-round service that keeps the pool maintained while you are away, so it is ready when you come back.

Surprise Farms and Greer Ranch

Newer communities along the Bell Road and Waddell corridors. Larger lots, newer pools, and often more complex setups with spas, water features, and automation. The water in this area tends to run hard — 400 to 600 ppm calcium — and salt cells need more frequent descaling than the manufacturer's timeline.

Prairie Springs and Central Surprise

Established neighborhoods closer to Litchfield Road and Grand Avenue. Older pools with aging equipment that benefit most from the weekly inspection — a worn pump seal caught at year 10 costs a fraction of a catastrophic motor failure at year 13.

Snowbird Pools

Surprise has a large seasonal population — homeowners who spend the summer elsewhere and return in October or November. If you leave for part of the year, we offer year-round service that keeps the pool chemically balanced, the equipment running, and the filter clean while you are gone. When you come back, the pool is ready to swim — no green-water cleanup, no emergency call, no surprise repair bill from equipment that sat idle in 115-degree heat.
