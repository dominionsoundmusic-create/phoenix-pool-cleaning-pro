Phoenix Pool Cleaning Pro is a FREE PHONE LINE and website, NOT a pool company. It has no
technicians, trucks, chemicals or equipment, and never cleans, repairs, quotes or schedules
anything itself. When someone calls:
- An automated assistant answers, any hour, and asks briefly what is going on with the pool.
