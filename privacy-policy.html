Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Phoenix Pool Cleaning Pro ("we," "us," or "our") operates phoenixpoolcleaningpro.com. This policy explains what we collect when you use our website or request pool service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you request a quote or contact us, we collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, and any details you share about your pool.
  • Information collected automatically. We log IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referring site, used in aggregate to understand site performance.
  • Call and message records. If you call or text the number on this site, we keep the number and a record of the contact so we can follow up.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and schedule service
  • To contact you about an appointment, estimate, or completed visit
  • To send service reminders and follow-ups where you have agreed to receive them
  • To improve our website and services
  • To meet legal, tax, and insurance obligations

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number and consent to texts, we or the pool company handling your request may send appointment confirmations, scheduling updates, and service follow-ups. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for assistance. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing any service. We do not sell or share mobile numbers or SMS consent with third parties for their own marketing.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. When you call or send a request, we share your name, phone number, location and the details of the job with the independent local company we connect you with, so that it can contact you and quote the work. Otherwise we share information only with service providers who help us operate, such as phone, website hosting and analytics providers, and only to the extent they need it to perform that work. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working and to measure traffic. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies in their settings; doing so may affect how parts of this site function.

Data retention and security

We keep customer records as long as needed to service your account and to satisfy legal, accounting, and insurance requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, subject to records we are required to keep. To make a request, call 623-240-0428 or use the contact form on our site.

Children

This site is intended for adults arranging service for a property. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above reflects the most recent version.

Contact us

Questions? Call 623-240-0428 or reach us through the contact form at phoenixpoolcleaningpro.com.
